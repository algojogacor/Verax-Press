# Content brief

## Reader task and unique value

Audience; decision/problem; why this page should exist; what it adds beyond existing content.

## Page strategy

New or revised URL; existing overlap; intended canonical; page type; relevant internal links. No invented search-volume estimate.

## Evidence needed

Facts, sources, original examples, testing records, media rights, author/reviewer responsibility and unresolved claims. Do not invent first-hand experience.

## Proposed outline

Direct answer → necessary background → explanation → evidence/example → limitations → useful next step. Include only sections that serve the task.

## Metadata and media

Descriptive title and heading; useful meta description; media role and accurate alt text. No mandatory character-count or keyword-density ritual.

## Acceptance and measurement

Factual review complete; claims supported; reader can perform the intended task; publication separately authorised; measured outcome and comparison window defined where data exists.
