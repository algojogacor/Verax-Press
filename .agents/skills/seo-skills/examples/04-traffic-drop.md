# Worked example: a drop is not a cause

**All numbers are invented for teaching.** Comparable report scopes show:

| Metric | Period A | Period B | Change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Impressions | 100,000 | 80,000 | -20.0% |
| Clicks | 3,000 | 1,600 | -46.7% |
| CTR | 3.0% | 2.0% | -1.0 percentage point |

CTR = clicks / impressions. The click ratio is the impression ratio multiplied by the CTR ratio: `0.8 × (2/3) = 0.5333…`. This arithmetic describes the movement; it does not prove why demand or click behavior changed.
