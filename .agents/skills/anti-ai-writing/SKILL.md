# Anti-AI Writing & Copywriting

> Rules and guidelines for writing, copywriting, and editing prose that sounds authentically human, clear, and engaging—without the recognizable patterns, buzzwords, and artificial cadences of AI-generated text (*AI slop*).

---

## 1. Core Principles

1. **Human Voice Over Machine Perfection**
   Authentic writing has personality, rhythm variations, and grounded clarity. Do not write text that is sterilized, generic, or puffed up with inflated adjectives.

2. **Never Invent Facts or Claims**
   Do not fabricate statistics (e.g. *"99.9% uptime"*, *"trusted by 10,000+ teams"*), fake testimonials, non-existent customer names, or unverified compliance claims. If real data is not available, state what the product or subject does directly or omit the claim entirely.

3. **Say the Thing Directly**
   Avoid throat-clearing, meta-commentary, and unnecessary preamble. Cut straight to the point.

---

## 2. Empty AI Vocabulary (BANNED)

AI models frequently overuse specific abstract verbs and nouns to sound profound or enthusiastic without adding real substance.

### Words to Avoid:
- **Verbs:** *delve, unlock, elevate, empower, harness, foster, streamline, revolutionize, showcase, usher in, spearhead, navigate, transcend*
- **Adjectives / Nouns:** *seamless, cutting-edge, robust, next-level, game-changer, pivotal, testament, tapestry, beacon, paramount, bespoke, multifaceted, crucial, dynamic*
- **Abstract Containers:** *journey, landscape (as a metaphor), realm, sphere, arena, horizon*

### Examples:
- ❌ **AI Slop:** *"Unlock the power of seamless collaboration to elevate your team's journey to the next level."*
- ✅ **Human:** *"Work with your team in one shared space."*

- ❌ **AI Slop:** *"This platform stands as a testament to cutting-edge innovation, ushering in a new era of productivity."*
- ✅ **Human:** *"This tool cuts the time teams spend on manual status reports."*

---

## 3. Rhythm & Structural Tells

AI prose tends to fall into repetitive sentence structures and formulaic cadences. Avoid these patterns:

### A. Forced "Rule of Three"
- ❌ **AI:** *"We deliver innovation, inspiration, and insights for modern workflows."*
- ✅ **Human:** *"We provide practical guides and real-world case studies for workflow automation."*

### B. Negative Parallelism
- ❌ **AI:** *"It's not just a tool; it's a game-changer for your workflow."*
- ❌ **AI:** *"Not only does it save time, but it also transforms how you work."*
- ✅ **Human:** *"It automates the repetitive parts of your day."*

### C. Aphorism Formulas
- ❌ **AI:** *"Communication is the lifeblood of collaboration. In today's fast-paced world, speed is the ultimate currency."*
- ✅ **Human:** *"Teams move faster when decisions are documented clearly."*

### D. Staccato Drama (Manufactured Punchiness)
- ❌ **AI:** *"No templates. No defaults. No limits. Just pure speed."*
- ✅ **Human:** *"Build from a blank canvas with complete design control."*

### E. False Ranges
- ❌ **AI:** *"From first click to final delivery, and everything in between."*
- ✅ **Human:** *"Handles ordering, payment, and shipment tracking."*

### F. Synonym Cycling
- ❌ **AI:** *"The checkout is fast. The purchasing process is quick. The payment flow is speedy."*
- ✅ **Human:** *"The checkout takes three clicks."*

## 4. Tone, Openers, & Closers

### A. Eliminate Signposting & Throat-Clearing
Do not announce what you are about to say.
- ❌ **AI:** *"Let's dive in."* / *"Here's what you need to know."* / *"Without further ado..."* / *"In this article, we will explore..."*
- ✅ **Human:** Start immediately with the first core idea.

### B. Eliminate Fake-Candid Openers
- ❌ **AI:** *"Honestly?"* / *"Let's be honest."* / *"Here's the thing:"* / *"Real talk:"*
- ✅ **Human:** Make the statement directly without conversational theater.

### C. Eliminate Chatbot Artifacts
Never include assistant-like closers in deliverables or copy.
- ❌ **AI:** *"I hope this helps!"* / *"Let me know if you have any questions!"* / *"Feel free to reach out if you need further assistance."*
- ✅ **Human:** End naturally on the last substantive point or a clear call to action.

### D. Weasel Attributions & Unearned Authority
- ❌ **AI:** *"Experts agree that this approach fundamentally shifts paradigms."*
- ✅ **Human:** Cite a specific source with name and date, or describe the mechanism directly without vague authorities.

## 5. Formatting & Markdown Hygiene

### A. Em Dash (`—`) Ban
The em dash is the single most common AI writing fingerprint.
- **Rule:** Do not use the em dash (`—`) or double hyphens (`--`).
- **Fix:** Use commas, periods (split into two sentences), colons, or parentheses instead.
- ❌ **AI:** *"The new update — released earlier this week — improves build speed."*
- ✅ **Human:** *"The new update, released earlier this week, improves build speed."*

### B. No Decorative Emojis in Headings & Bullet Lists
Avoid putting decorative emojis at the start of every heading or bullet point (e.g., 🚀 *Launch*, 💡 *Insight*, ✅ *Feature*). Use clean typography.

### C. No Mechanical Boldface Overuse
Do not bold every second keyword or phrase in a sentence. Bold text should only be used for true structural emphasis or defined terms.
- ❌ **AI:** *"It integrates with **GitHub**, **GitLab**, and **Bitbucket** to automate **CI/CD pipelines**."*
- ✅ **Human:** *"It integrates with GitHub, GitLab, and Bitbucket to automate CI/CD pipelines."*

### D. No Tautological Inline Headers
Avoid bullet lists where a bold prefix simply repeats what follows:
- ❌ **AI:**
  - **User Experience:** The user experience is enhanced.
  - **Performance:** Performance has been optimized.
- ✅ **Human:**
  - Faster load times across all dashboards.
  - Simpler navigation with fewer required clicks.

### E. Cut Filler Phrases & Excessive Hedging
- Replace *"In order to"* with *"To"*.
- Replace *"Due to the fact that"* with *"Because"*.
- Replace *"It is important to note that"* with nothing (delete it).
- Replace *"could potentially possibly be"* with *"may be"*.

---

## 6. Characteristics of Authentic Human Writing

When writing or editing, aim for these traits:

1. **Burstiness (Sentence Variety):** Mix short, punchy sentences with longer, detailed explanations. AI tends toward uniform, mid-length sentences.
2. **Concrete Details:** Use specific examples, real trade-offs, and grounded explanations rather than high-level abstractions.
3. **Active Voice & Named Actors:** Make it clear who did what (e.g., *"We updated the API"* instead of *"The API was updated"*).
4. **Natural Cadence:** Read the text out loud. If it sounds like a corporate press release or a chatbot response, rewrite it.

---

## 7. Pre-Delivery Checklist

Before finalizing any text, verify:
- [ ] No empty AI buzzwords (*delve, unlock, elevate, seamless, robust, etc.*).
- [ ] No em dashes (`—`).
- [ ] No fake metrics, fictional testimonials, or unevidenced trust claims.
- [ ] No forced rule-of-three, negative parallelism, or fake-candid openers.
- [ ] No decorative heading emojis or mechanical keyword bolding.
- [ ] No chatbot closing remarks (*"I hope this helps"*).
- [ ] Sentence lengths vary naturally and read well aloud.
